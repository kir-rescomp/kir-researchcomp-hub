# Isambard-AI

KIR holds a rolling, **institute-wide allocation** on Isambard-AI under the project
*Exploratory Evaluation of Single-Cell Genomics and Spatial Transcriptomics*.
The current allocation is active from **5 November 2026** for three months, and
researchers from any KIR group are welcome to join.

<div class="grid cards info-cards" markdown>

-   :material-information-outline:{ .lg .middle } __What is Isambard-AI?__

    ---

    The UK's fastest and most powerful AI supercomputer, operated by the
    University of Bristol, and one of two national AI facilities alongside
    Zenith (University of Cambridge).

-   :material-harddisk:{ .lg .middle } __How much storage is available?__

    ---

    **200 TB** of shared project storage, plus **5 TB** of private storage for
    each individual user account.

-   :material-chip:{ .lg .middle } __What can it be used for?__

    ---

    **Large-scale distributed GPU workloads**, with a single job able to request
    up to several thousand GPUs. With GPUs readily available through a dedicated
    reservation, it is also an **ideal environment for building GPU and CUDA
    skills**, from profiling code to porting CPU-based workflows to GPU.

-   :material-cash-remove:{ .lg .middle } __Is there an additional cost?__

    ---

    **None.** Administration and local support for the Isambard-AI environment,
    including software, are covered through the SRF. This single allocation
    serves the whole institute, so groups need not apply individually.

</div>

## Moving data between Isambard-AI and BMRC

- **General research data** can be transferred between Isambard-AI and the BMRC
  cluster without restriction.

!!! warning "Sensitive data"

    The allocation is not yet cleared for sensitive data. Although Isambard-AI
    is NHS-certified, it has not been reviewed by NDORMS Information Governance.
    Sensitive-data use cases can be discussed individually to identify an interim
    approach, while at least two projects are under way to deploy Trusted Research
    Environments (data safe havens) approved by both the UK Government and NDORMS.

## Access and further information

!!! tip "Request access"

    Please contact KIR Research Computing Manager for further information or to request access.
    An account will be created for you, and you will be added to the KIR
    Isambard-AI Microsoft Teams channel, where technical questions can be raised
    and discussed.
